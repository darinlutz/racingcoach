# Ferrari 296 GT3 — drawing notes (DRAFT, unverified)
Source model: "Ferrari 296 GT3" by MattDoesBlender (https://sketchfab.com/MattDoesBlender), CC BY 4.0,
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ferrari-296-gt3-e6760847bb2d4b579d43cf916f348526 (licence confirmed by owner).
Same pipeline and settings style as the approved Huracán GT3 EVO set. 1 SVG unit = 1 mm.

- The model sat at an angle (yawed ~37°); it was straightened using the four tyre centres.
- Scaled so wheelbase = 2660 mm (published figure, not checked against homologation papers). Model's own wheelbase was 2660 mm before scaling, so scale ≈ 1.0.
- From model: length 4743, height 1188, width 2369 incl. mirrors, front/rear track 1696/1690, tyres 678/702 mm.
- Removed: roof antennas and two tiny shadow/label planes.
- Small stepped squares on the door/side are in the model itself.
- Orientation: left side = front screen-left; right side = front screen-right; top = front up, car-left screen-left; front = car-left on screen-RIGHT; rear = car-left on screen-left.
