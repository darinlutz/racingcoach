Known limitations
- Model internals carry names from a racing-sim mod ("rss_gtm_lanzo_v10", "RSS_", "CSP_GLASS"), i.e. it may originate from Race Sim Studio's fictionalised car for Assetto Corsa. CC BY was applied by the uploader; confirm the uploader had the right to license it.
- No verified orthographic drawing or licensed reference photo was used; geometry is NOT labelled verified.
- Length mismatch may come from the splitter/wing extent or model scale error; check against your reference.
- Ride-height reference = lowest bodywork point within 400 mm of each axle.
